About Us

Professional electrical assistance for urgent problems and everyday electrical needs.

Electrical help when it can't wait

Pro Electrician provides emergency and everyday electrical assistance for homes and commercial properties. Electrical problems rarely arrive at a convenient time, so we focus on making it simple to reach a professional, explain what's happening, and get a clear path to a fix.

We help with urgent faults such as tripping breakers, power loss inside a property, damaged wiring and outlets that smell of burning, as well as panel, wiring and troubleshooting work.

Safety first

Every job starts with making the situation safe for you, your property and anyone working on it.

Clear communication

You'll know what the problem is, what the work involves and what happens next.

How we work

  • You get in touch. Call us, or send the details through the contact form.
  • We discuss the problem. We ask what you've seen, heard or smelled and which parts of the property are affected.
  • We assess the issue. The fault is assessed to determine the right solution.
  • We complete the agreed work with safety and quality in mind.
