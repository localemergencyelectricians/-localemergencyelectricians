Multifamily Electrical — Local Emergency Electrician

Apartment and condo buildings have electrical needs that single homes do not: meter banks, shared panels, common-area lighting and dozens of units with different problems. We provide multifamily electrical services for property managers and owners, including unit repairs, meter bank and panel upgrades, common-area lighting, EV chargers and code corrections. When a tenant reports a dead outlet or a tripping breaker, the fix needs to happen quickly and without disturbing the rest of the building. And when a building's meter bank or main service is ageing, the upgrade has to be planned carefully so tenants are not left without power for longer than necessary.

We work with property managers, HOAs and building owners to keep multifamily properties safe and compliant. That means responsive unit repairs, planned upgrades, clear communication with tenants, and work documented for your records.

What’s included

Our multifamily electrical services include:

  • Unit repairs, including outlets, switches, lighting and breaker faults
  • Meter bank and main service upgrades for ageing buildings
  • Panel upgrades for individual units and common areas
  • Common-area, hallway, stairwell and parking lighting
  • EV charger installation for shared parking and individual units
  • Code corrections following inspections
  • Smoke and CO detector installation and replacement across units
  • Turnover electrical checks between tenants

Signs you need multifamily electrical

Your building may need electrical work if:

  • Tenants report recurring outlet, switch or breaker problems
  • The meter bank or main service is old, corroded or at capacity
  • Common-area lighting is dim, flickering or failing
  • Tenants are asking for EV charging in the parking area
  • An inspection has flagged electrical code issues
  • You need reliable electrical support for unit turnovers

How it works

Multifamily work is organised around tenants and building schedules:

  1. 1. Call and describe the work. Tell us about the building, the number of units and the work needed, whether it is a single unit repair or a building-wide upgrade.
  2. 2. Site visit and quote. We inspect the panels, meter bank and affected areas, then give you an upfront price and a schedule that minimises tenant disruption.
  3. 3. Coordinated work. We coordinate access with tenants through your office, work unit by unit where possible, and keep common areas safe while we work.
  4. 4. Documentation. We provide a clear record of the work done, including any inspection paperwork, for your building files.

Safety first

Emergency? If there is fire, smoke, or anyone is in danger, leave the building and call 911 (your local emergency number) first. Keep away from sparking equipment and never touch exposed wires.

Multifamily buildings carry a responsibility to every tenant. Faulty wiring in one unit can affect the whole building, and common-area faults put everyone at risk. If a tenant reports a burning smell, sparking or shocks, treat it as urgent and call an electrician promptly.

We never work on a live meter bank or main service without coordinating a safe shut-off with the utility and the building. Tenant safety and clear communication come first on every multifamily job.

Frequently asked questions

Can you work with our property management team?

Yes. We coordinate access, scheduling and tenant communication through your office, and we provide documentation of the work for your building records.

How do you handle access to occupied units?

We schedule visits through your office and work to the access arrangements you set with tenants. An adult, or your authorised representative, needs to be present for unit work.

Can you install EV chargers in an apartment parking area?

Yes. We install shared and individual EV chargers for multifamily parking, including load management so the building's service is not overloaded, and metering options where needed.

What is a meter bank upgrade?

A meter bank is the set of meters and disconnects serving all the units in a building. Older meter banks can corrode, run out of capacity or fail inspection. An upgrade replaces the bank with modern equipment sized for the building.

Do you help with inspection code corrections?

Yes. We correct flagged electrical issues, from missing GFCI protection and unlabeled panels to grounding problems, and we can provide documentation for the re-inspection.
