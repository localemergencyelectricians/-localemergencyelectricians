Electrical services across Rhode Island

Our licensed electricians offer these services throughout Rhode Island:

Electrical Repairs

Fast diagnosis and repair of power outages, tripping breakers, dead outlets, flickering lights, burning smells and faulty wiring, including emergency callouts.

Electrical Panel Upgrades

Replace outdated or overloaded breaker panels with 100A, 200A or larger service for safer power, more capacity and room for EV chargers and additions.

Electrical Wiring and Rewiring

New circuits, 240V wiring, garage and detached-building wiring, and whole-home rewiring to replace old knob-and-tube or aluminum wiring.

EV Charger Installation

Home Level 2 EV charger installation for Tesla and other EVs, with dedicated 240V circuits, permits and panel upgrades when needed.

Generator Services

Standby and whole-home generator installation, transfer switches, wiring, maintenance and repair to keep your power on during outages.

Surge Protection

Whole-house surge protectors installed at your panel to shield appliances, electronics and wiring from voltage spikes and lightning.

Lighting and Electrical Fixtures

Recessed lighting, outdoor and landscape lighting, chandeliers, ceiling fans and dimmers installed or repaired for better light and lower energy use.

Switches and Outlets

Install, replace or repair switches and outlets, including GFCI and AFCI protection, USB outlets, dimmers and smart switches.
