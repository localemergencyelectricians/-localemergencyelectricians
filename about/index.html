About Us

Professional electrical assistance for urgent problems and everyday electrical needs.

Electrical help when it can’t wait

Local Emergency Electrician provides emergency and everyday electrical assistance for homes and commercial properties. Electrical problems rarely arrive at a convenient time, so we focus on making it simple to reach a professional, explain what’s happening, and get a clear path to a fix. We help with urgent faults such as tripping breakers, power loss inside a property, damaged wiring and outlets that smell of burning, as well as panel, wiring and troubleshooting work.

The company was founded in Los Angeles, California, in 2011 by Jennifer, and is headquartered at 2701 S Figueroa St, Los Angeles, CA 90007. Local Emergency Electrician is also known as Pro Electrician and Electric Pro — the same company, the same phone number, and the same team.

How we serve the whole country

We are a Los Angeles company, and our service area has grown far beyond it. Today we serve 10,633 cities across 49 states and the District of Columbia. Services outside the Los Angeles area are delivered through contractor agreements with local licensed electricians, so the person who arrives at your door is a licensed professional who knows your area and its local code. Wherever you are — from Los Angeles to Texas, Florida, and New York — the process is the same: one phone number, one conversation, upfront pricing, and work done to code.

Residential & commercial

How we work

  • You get in touch. Call us at (844) 957-4676, Monday–Saturday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, or send the details through the contact form.
  • We discuss the problem. We ask what you’ve seen, heard or smelled and which parts of the property are affected.
  • We assess the issue. The fault is assessed to determine the right solution, and we explain the upfront price.
  • We complete the agreed work with safety and quality in mind, test it, and leave the area tidy.
