What happens after you send the form?

Your request goes straight to our team. We read what you sent, and we call you back at the number you gave to discuss the problem, ask any follow-up questions, and arrange the next step — usually an assessment visit by a licensed electrician. If you marked your request as an emergency, calling us directly at (844) 957-4676 is still the fastest option, because a form reply takes longer than a phone call.
