Electrical Emergencies Should Never Be Ignored

Electrical issues can create serious safety concerns. Recognizing warning signs and seeking professional assistance can help protect your property and the people inside it.

  • Repeated circuit breaker trips.
  • Burning smells near electrical outlets.
  • Flickering lights and sudden power interruptions.
  • Sparking outlets or unusual electrical sounds.
