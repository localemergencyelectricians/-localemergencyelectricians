Common electrical problems in New Lisbon

These are the electrical problems we are called out for most often in New Lisbon. If anything below sounds familiar, call us before a small fault turns into a bigger one.

EV charger or new appliance circuit

Level 2 EV chargers, induction cooktops, and heat-pump dryers all need a dedicated 240-volt circuit — and often a panel with spare capacity. In New Lisbon, we install the circuit, the charger, and any panel work needed, and handle the permit paperwork where required.

What to do: Tell us the charger or appliance model when you call, and we will confirm what your panel can support and give you upfront pricing.

Breaker keeps tripping

A breaker that trips again and again usually means an overloaded circuit, a short circuit, or a ground fault — not just a faulty breaker. In New Lisbon homes, the usual culprits are space heaters, window air conditioners, or too many high-draw appliances sharing one circuit. Repeated tripping is your electrical system asking for help, and ignoring it can damage wiring over time.

What to do: Unplug what you can from the affected circuit, reset the breaker once, and if it trips again, leave it off and call us. We will find the cause and fix it properly.

Flickering or dimming lights

Lights that flicker when the air conditioner or microwave kicks on often point to a loose connection, an overloaded circuit, or a panel that cannot keep up with demand. It can also be a sign of worn wiring in older New Lisbon homes. Occasional flicker in one fixture may just be a loose bulb, but flicker across several rooms deserves a professional look.

What to do: Note which lights flicker and when it happens, then call (844) 957-4676. We will check the circuit, the connections, and the panel.
