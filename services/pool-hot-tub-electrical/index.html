Pool and Hot Tub Electrical — Local Emergency Electrician

Water and electricity are a dangerous combination, which is why pool and hot tub wiring has some of the strictest code requirements of any electrical work. A pool or hot tub needs power for pumps, filters, heaters, lights and controls, and every part of that system sits close to water. Code requires GFCI protection on pool and spa circuits, equipotential bonding of metal parts, and specific clearances for outlets and lighting. Getting any of it wrong puts swimmers at risk.

We wire new pools and hot tubs, hook up replacement equipment, repair underwater lights and pumps, and bring older installations up to current code. Every job is tested before the pool or spa is used again.

What’s included

Our pool and hot tub electrical services include:

  • New pool and spa wiring, including dedicated circuits for pumps and heaters
  • Hot tub hookups, including 240V circuits and disconnects
  • GFCI protection for pool, spa and surrounding outlets
  • Equipotential bonding of pool shells, ladders, rails and equipment
  • Underwater lighting installation and repair
  • Pump, filter and heater wiring and replacement hookups
  • Timers, automation and control wiring for pool equipment
  • Safety inspections and code upgrades for older pool installations

Signs you need pool and hot tub electrical

Call an electrician if you notice any of these around your pool or hot tub:

  • You feel a tingle or shock when touching the water, rails or ladder
  • The GFCI for the pool or hot tub trips repeatedly, or will not reset
  • Underwater lights are out, flickering or showing water inside the fitting
  • The pump, heater or controls have stopped working
  • You are installing a new hot tub and need a 240V circuit and disconnect
  • Your pool wiring is old, or you are unsure whether it meets current code

How it works

Pool and spa work follows a careful process:

  1. 1. Assessment. We inspect the existing wiring, bonding and GFCI protection, or plan the new installation, including the panel capacity and circuit route.
  2. 2. Quote and permits. You get an upfront price. Pool and spa work usually needs a permit and an inspection, which we handle where required.
  3. 3. Installation or repair. We run the circuits, fit the GFCI protection and disconnects, bond the metal parts and connect the equipment.
  4. 4. Testing before use. We test GFCI protection, verify bonding and check every piece of equipment before the pool or spa goes back into use.

Safety first

Emergency? If there is fire, smoke, or anyone is in danger, leave the building and call 911 (your local emergency number) first. Keep away from sparking equipment and never touch exposed wires.

A tingling sensation in pool water is a serious warning sign. It can mean stray voltage from faulty wiring or failed bonding. Get everyone out of the water immediately, switch the pool equipment off at the panel, and call an electrician before anyone swims again.

Never handle pool electrical equipment with wet hands or while standing in water, and never bypass a GFCI that keeps tripping. A tripping GFCI is protecting you from a real fault, and overriding it removes that protection.

Frequently asked questions

Does a hot tub need a dedicated circuit?

Yes. Most hot tubs need a dedicated 240V circuit, typically 40A to 60A, with GFCI protection and a disconnect within sight of the tub. We size the circuit to your specific hot tub model.

What is pool bonding, and why does it matter?

Bonding connects all the metal parts around a pool, such as ladders, rails, the pump and the shell, so they are all at the same electrical potential. Without it, a fault can create a voltage difference that shocks swimmers.

Why does my pool GFCI keep tripping?

The usual causes are moisture in a junction box or light fitting, a failing pump motor, or a worn-out GFCI. We test the circuit and each piece of equipment to find the fault.

Can you repair underwater pool lights?

Yes. We replace lamps and seals, repair leaking light niches and rewire underwater lighting where needed. Lights are always tested with GFCI protection in place.

Do I need a permit for hot tub wiring?

In most areas, yes. A new hot tub circuit needs a permit and an inspection. We handle the paperwork and arrange the inspection where required.
