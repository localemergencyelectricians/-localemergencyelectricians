Smart Home Electrical — Local Emergency Electrician

A smart home starts with the wiring behind the walls. Smart switches, dimmers, lighting control and security cameras all need the right power and connections to work reliably. We install and wire smart home electrical upgrades, from a single smart switch to whole-home lighting control and camera wiring. Call (844) 957-4676 to plan your smart home.

Smart home devices promise convenience, but they only work well when the electrical side is right. A smart switch in a box with no neutral wire will not work at all. A camera on a weak power supply drops out at the worst moment. And a house full of smart bulbs on old dimmers flickers and buzzes.

We handle the electrical foundation: smart switches and dimmers wired correctly, circuits for cameras and doorbells, lighting control that works with your chosen platform, and the neutral wiring and panel capacity that smart homes need.

What’s included

Our smart home electrical services include:

  • Smart switches and dimmers, including neutral-wire installation where needed
  • Whole-home lighting control and scene setup with your chosen platform
  • Security camera wiring and power, including PoE runs
  • Video doorbell wiring, transformers and chime connections
  • Smart thermostat wiring, including common-wire (C-wire) installation
  • Automated blinds and shade wiring
  • Dedicated circuits for hubs, network equipment and home theatre
  • Automation-ready wiring for new builds, additions and remodels

Signs you need smart home electrical

You may need smart home electrical work if:

  • You want voice or app control of your lights, but your switches are old
  • A smart switch you bought will not work because there is no neutral wire
  • Your security cameras keep dropping out or rely on batteries
  • Your video doorbell chime does not work, or the transformer is too weak
  • Your smart thermostat reports a missing C-wire
  • You are building or remodelling and want the home wired for automation

How it works

Smart home work follows a simple process:

  1. 1. Talk through your plans. Tell us which platform you use, or want to use, and what you want to control. We will advise on the devices and wiring that fit.
  2. 2. On-site check and quote. We check the switch boxes, wiring and Wi-Fi coverage in the areas involved, then give you an upfront price.
  3. 3. Installation and wiring. We fit the switches, dimmers and wiring, run camera and doorbell cables, and connect everything neatly.
  4. 4. Setup and walkthrough. We test each device, help you connect it to your app or hub, and show you how to use scenes and schedules.

Safety first

Emergency? If there is fire, smoke, or anyone is in danger, leave the building and call 911 (your local emergency number) first. Keep away from sparking equipment and never touch exposed wires.

Smart switches carry mains voltage just like any other switch, so they must be installed with the power off and wired to the correct terminals. A smart switch wired without a proper neutral, or with line and load reversed, can damage the device or create a shock risk. We verify every connection before powering up.

Outdoor cameras and doorbells need weatherproof fittings and, where they are mains powered, GFCI protection. We install them to the same standards as any other outdoor electrical work.

Frequently asked questions

Do smart switches need a neutral wire?

Most do. The neutral powers the switch's own electronics. Many older homes do not have a neutral in the switch box, but we can usually run one, or recommend a smart switch designed to work without it.

Will smart switches work with my voice assistant?

Most smart switches work with the major platforms, including Alexa, Google Home and Apple HomeKit. Tell us which platform you use and we will recommend compatible devices.

Can you wire security cameras?

Yes. We run power and data cabling for wired cameras, including PoE runs, and we can install a dedicated circuit for your camera system and network equipment.

Can you install a smart thermostat?

Yes, including running a C-wire where one is missing. A C-wire gives the thermostat constant power, which most smart thermostats need to work reliably.

Do I need to replace all my switches at once?

No. Many people start with the rooms they use most, such as the living room and bedrooms, and add more later. We can plan the work in stages so it fits your budget.
