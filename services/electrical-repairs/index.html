Electrical faults rarely fix themselves, and the longer they go unchecked, the more damage they can do. A breaker that trips every evening, a flickering light or a dead outlet all mean something in your wiring, panel or devices needs attention from a licensed electrician.

Every repair visit starts with proper diagnosis: we test the circuits, check the panel and trace the fault, then explain what we found in plain language before any work begins. Most common repairs are finished in a single visit.

What’s included

Every repair visit covers diagnosis, the repair and a safety check of the circuit. Common repairs include:

  • Breaker troubleshooting and replacement, including breakers that trip repeatedly
  • Dead outlet and dead switch diagnosis and repair
  • Flickering, dimming or buzzing light repairs
  • Partial power loss to a room or part of the home
  • Burning smell or scorch-mark investigation at outlets, switches and panels
  • Faulty or damaged wiring repair, including loose connections
  • GFCI and AFCI outlet testing, resetting and replacement
  • Emergency callouts for urgent faults, including sparking and outages

Signs you need electrical repairs

Call an electrician promptly if you notice any of these warning signs:

  • A breaker trips again soon after you reset it
  • Outlets or switch plates feel warm, or show scorch marks or discoloration
  • Lights flicker or dim when another appliance switches on
  • A burning or fishy smell near an outlet, switch or your electrical panel
  • Buzzing, crackling or popping sounds from outlets, switches or the panel
  • A shock or tingle when you touch an appliance or switch

How it works

Here is what happens when you book an electrical repair with us:

  1. 1. Call and describe the problem. Tell us what is happening and when it started. We will ask a few questions, advise on anything you should switch off, and arrange a visit.
  2. 2. Diagnosis on site. The electrician tests the circuits, inspects the panel and traces the fault, then explains what is wrong.
  3. 3. Upfront quote before work starts. You get a clear price for the repair before any work begins, so there are no surprises on the bill.
  4. 4. Repair and test. We complete the repair, test the circuit under load and check the surrounding wiring.

Safety first

Emergency? If there is fire, smoke, or anyone is in danger, leave the building and call 911 (your local emergency number) first. Keep away from sparking equipment and never touch exposed wires.

Never keep resetting a breaker that trips again and again. A tripping breaker is doing its job by cutting power to a fault, and forcing it back on can overheat wiring. If an outlet is sparking or you smell burning, switch the circuit off at the panel if you can do so safely, and keep away from the affected area.

Do not open a panel or pull out an outlet yourself. Even with a breaker off, a panel can still carry live power. Our electricians test to confirm a circuit is dead before touching it.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if my breaker keeps tripping?

Unplug the appliance on that circuit and reset the breaker once. If it trips again, leave it off and call an electrician. Repeated tripping usually means an overloaded circuit, a short circuit or a failing breaker, and forcing it back on can overheat the wiring.

Is a burning smell from an outlet an emergency?

Yes, treat it as urgent. Switch the circuit off at the panel if you can do so safely, keep away from the outlet and call us right away. If there is smoke or fire, leave the building and call 911 first.

How fast can an electrician come out?

We work Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and urgent faults are prioritised. Call (844) 957-4676 for the earliest available time.

Do I need to be home for a repair visit?

Yes, an adult needs to be present so the electrician can access the panel and affected rooms.

Will the repair fix the problem for good?

We repair the fault itself, not just the symptom, and test the circuit before we leave. If it points to a bigger issue, such as an overloaded panel, we will explain your options first.
