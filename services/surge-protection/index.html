Surge Protection — Local Emergency Electrician

A single voltage spike can destroy a television, a computer or an appliance in an instant, and smaller surges wear electronics down over time. A whole-house surge protector, installed at your electrical panel, shields every circuit in your home from spikes caused by lightning, grid switching and large appliances. Most people know to use a power strip with surge protection for a computer, but a strip only protects what is plugged into it. Your fridge, washer, HVAC system and oven have expensive electronics too, and they are wired straight to your panel with no protection at all.

A whole-house surge protective device sits at the panel and diverts excess voltage safely to ground before it can reach any circuit. It works alongside point-of-use protection for your most valuable electronics, giving your home layered protection against spikes.

What’s included

Our surge protection services include:

  • Whole-house surge protective device (SPD) installed at your main panel
  • Panel assessment to choose the right SPD rating for your service
  • Grounding check, because surge protection only works with a sound ground
  • Point-of-use surge protection advice for computers and home theatre
  • SPD replacement for units that have done their job and worn out
  • Surge protection for subpanels in garages, workshops and additions
  • Protection for EV chargers, HVAC systems and smart home equipment
  • Testing and status checks after storms or power events

Signs you need surge protection

Your home may need surge protection if:

  • You live in an area with frequent thunderstorms or lightning
  • You have lost electronics or appliances to a power surge before
  • Your lights flicker or electronics restart when large appliances switch on
  • Your home has expensive electronics, a home theatre or a home office
  • You have an EV charger, smart home devices or a modern HVAC system
  • Your panel has no surge protective device fitted at all

How it works

Installing whole-house surge protection is quick and straightforward:

  1. 1. Panel and grounding check. We inspect your panel, confirm there is space for the device and check that your grounding is sound.
  2. 2. Upfront quote. We recommend the right SPD rating for your service size and give you a clear price before work starts.
  3. 3. Installation. The device is wired into the panel, usually within an hour, with minimal disruption to your power.
  4. 4. Test and explain. We verify the device is active, show you its status lights and explain what to watch for after a storm.

Safety first

Emergency? If there is fire, smoke, or anyone is in danger, leave the building and call 911 (your local emergency number) first. Keep away from sparking equipment and never touch exposed wires.

Surge protection depends on good grounding. If your home's grounding is damaged, corroded or missing, a surge protector cannot divert excess voltage safely. We check grounding as part of every installation and will tell you if it needs attention first.

No surge protector can guarantee protection against a direct lightning strike, but a whole-house device greatly reduces the risk from nearby strikes and grid surges, and it is the best single step you can take to protect your home's electronics.

Frequently asked questions

Is whole-house surge protection better than a power strip?

They do different jobs. A whole-house device protects every circuit in your home, including hardwired appliances. A power strip only protects what is plugged into it. The best protection is layered: a whole-house device plus strips for your most valuable electronics.

Does surge protection work against lightning?

It greatly reduces the risk from nearby lightning strikes and the surges they cause on power lines. No device can guarantee protection against a direct strike on your home, but a whole-house SPD is the strongest practical defence available.

How long does a surge protector last?

An SPD wears out gradually as it absorbs surges. Most have status lights that show when they need replacing. We recommend checking the status after major storms and replacing a unit that shows it has done its job.

How long does installation take?

Usually under an hour, once the panel and grounding have been checked. Power is off only briefly while the device is connected.

Will surge protection stop my breakers tripping?

No, that is a different problem. Surge protectors guard against voltage spikes, while breakers guard against overloads and short circuits. If your breakers trip often, you may need a panel assessment or an extra circuit.
