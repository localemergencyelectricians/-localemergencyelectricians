How to choose the right service

Something stopped working or smells wrong? Start with repairs and troubleshooting. Breakers trip when you run the dryer and the air conditioner together? Your panel may be undersized — see panel upgrades. Building, extending, or remodeling? New home, home addition, and remodeling electrical cover the full wiring plan. Adding an EV, a hot tub, or a workshop? Those need dedicated circuits — see EV charger installation, pool and hot tub electrical, and wiring. Still unsure? Call us and describe it; pointing you to the right service is part of the job.

How every job works
