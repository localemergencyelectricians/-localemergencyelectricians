Common electrical problems in Clackamas

These are the electrical problems we are called out for most often in Clackamas. If anything below sounds familiar, call us before a small fault turns into a bigger one.

Breaker keeps tripping

A breaker that trips again and again usually means an overloaded circuit, a short circuit, or a ground fault — not just a faulty breaker. In Clackamas homes, the usual culprits are space heaters, window air conditioners, or too many high-draw appliances sharing one circuit. Repeated tripping is your electrical system asking for help, and ignoring it can damage wiring over time.

What to do: Unplug what you can from the affected circuit, reset the breaker once, and if it trips again, leave it off and call us. We will find the cause and fix it properly.

Flickering or dimming lights

Lights that flicker when the air conditioner or microwave kicks on often point to a loose connection, an overloaded circuit, or a panel that cannot keep up with demand. It can also be a sign of worn wiring in older Clackamas homes. Occasional flicker in one fixture may just be a loose bulb, but flicker across several rooms deserves a professional look.

What to do: Note which lights flicker and when it happens, then call (844) 957-4676. We will check the circuit, the connections, and the panel.

Burning smell or warm outlets

A burning smell near an outlet or switch — or a switch plate that feels warm — is a warning sign that should never be ignored. It usually means a loose connection or an overloaded outlet is overheating behind the wall. This is one of the most common causes of electrical fires in homes.

What to do: Turn off power to that circuit at your breaker panel, do not use the outlet, and call us right away. If you see smoke or flames, leave the building and call 911 first.

What to do now

  1. Stay safe first. If there is fire, smoke, or anyone is in danger, leave the building and call 911 (your local emergency number) first. Keep away from sparking equipment and never touch exposed wires.
  2. If it is safe, switch off power to the affected circuit at your breaker panel and unplug appliances on that circuit.
  3. Make a note of what happened: which rooms lost power, which breaker tripped, and whether you noticed a smell, buzzing, or warmth at an outlet.
  4. Call (844) 957-4676, Monday–Saturday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM. Tell us what you noticed and we will arrange the next available visit in Clackamas — or request a quote through our contact page.
