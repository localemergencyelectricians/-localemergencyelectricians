Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Effective: 17 November 2026. Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Who we are

This website, localemergencyelectrician.com, is operated by Local Emergency Electrician (“we”, “us”). Our address is 2701 S Figueroa St, Los Angeles, CA 90007. You can reach us by phone at (844) 957-4676 or through our contact page.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address (if given), city or ZIP code, property type, how urgent the problem is, and your message. If you call us, we may note the details you give us. Our servers and hosting provider may also record technical data such as IP address and browser type in standard logs.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide quotes or services.
  • To arrange and carry out electrical work you request.
  • To keep records and protect against spam or misuse of the site.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Form processing by Formspree

Contact form submissions are processed by Formspree (formspree.io), which receives your submission and forwards it to us by email. Formspree’s handling of data is described in its own privacy policy.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a form script from unpkg.com, which may receive your IP address and browser details when your browser requests those files. We do not use analytics or advertising tools that collect personal information.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal requirements.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to certain uses. Contact us by phone at (844) 957-4676 or through our contact page and we’ll respond to your request.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Call (844) 957-4676 or use our contact page.
