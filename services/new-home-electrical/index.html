New Home Electrical — Local Emergency Electrician

Building a new home means getting the electrical system right from the start, because fixing it later is expensive. We handle complete electrical work for new construction, from load planning and panel setup through rough-in wiring, outlets and lighting, to final inspection support. A new home's electrical system has to serve the family for decades. That means planning for today's needs and tomorrow's: enough circuits for a modern kitchen, capacity for an EV charger, wiring for home offices and smart home devices, and a panel with room to grow.

We work with homeowners, builders and general contractors to deliver new home electrical on schedule. Our work is planned from the drawings, installed to code and inspected at each stage, so the final inspection goes smoothly.

What’s included

Our new home electrical service covers the full build:

  • Load planning and service sizing for the whole home
  • Panel and service installation, including meter base and grounding
  • Rough-in wiring for every room, including outlets, switches and lighting
  • Dedicated circuits for kitchen appliances, HVAC, laundry and workshops
  • Recessed, outdoor and decorative lighting installation
  • Hardwired smoke and CO detectors, as code requires
  • EV charger, generator and smart home pre-wiring
  • Final trim-out, device installation, testing and inspection support

Signs you need new home electrical

You need a new home electrician if you are:

  • Building a new home from the ground up
  • Rebuilding after a fire, flood or other major damage
  • Acting as your own general contractor and need a licensed electrician
  • Planning a custom home with specific lighting or smart home requirements
  • Building a detached guest house, barn or workshop with full power
  • Looking for an electrician who can work to your builder's schedule

How it works

New home electrical follows the build in clear stages:

  1. 1. Plan from the drawings. We review the plans with you and your builder, confirm outlet and lighting locations, and size the service for the home.
  2. 2. Rough-in. Once framing is up, we run all the wiring, fit boxes and install the panel, ready for the rough-in inspection.
  3. 3. Trim-out. After drywall and painting, we install outlets, switches, fixtures and detectors, and connect the panel.
  4. 4. Final testing and inspection. Every circuit is tested, the system is labelled, and we support the final inspection so you can move in on time.

Safety first

Emergency? If there is fire, smoke, or anyone is in danger, leave the building and call 911 (your local emergency number) first. Keep away from sparking equipment and never touch exposed wires.

New construction wiring is inspected at several stages, and for good reason. Wiring buried in walls is hard to fix later, so every connection must be right the first time. We use the correct cable and boxes for each location, protect wiring from nails and screws, and test every circuit before it is covered.

Temporary power during construction needs the same care as permanent wiring. We can set up safe temporary power for the build site so trades can work without overloading circuits or running extension cords through wet areas.

Frequently asked questions

When should the electrician start on a new build?

We get involved early, during planning, so the service size and outlet locations are right from the start. Rough-in wiring begins once framing is complete and the roof is on.

Do you work with my builder or general contractor?

Yes. We coordinate with your builder's schedule, attend site meetings as needed and work to the same inspection milestones as the rest of the build.

What is included in rough-in wiring?

Rough-in covers all the wiring inside the walls: cable runs, outlet and switch boxes, the panel and service, and the wiring for lighting, detectors and dedicated circuits. Trim-out, when devices and fixtures are fitted, comes after drywall.

Can you pre-wire for an EV charger or smart home?

Yes, and it is much cheaper to do during construction than afterwards. We can run the circuit for an EV charger, wire for smart switches and cameras, and add network cabling while the walls are open.

How long does new home electrical take?

It depends on the size of the home and the build schedule. Rough-in for a typical home takes one to two weeks, with trim-out taking a further week or so. We work to your builder's timeline.
